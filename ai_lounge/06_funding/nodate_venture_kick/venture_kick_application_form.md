# Venture Kick — Stage 1 Application

## Guidelines

- Fill out the form in **English**.
- All information is **confidential** — accessible only
  to the Stage 1 Jury.
- Sections marked 👁 **may become public** if you win
  Stage 1.
- Save at any time and resume via the **same link**
  sent to your email.
- Co-founders: mention in Step 3 "Team". If they hold
  the academic link, note it in Step 4 under
  "Academic Link".
- **Final submission**: go to Step 5 "Documents" →
  click **COMPLETE APPLICATION & SEND**.
- Questions: +41 (58) 767 00 02 / info@venturekick.ch

## Step 3 — Project Description

All fields below are free text. Character limits apply.

| Field | Prompt |
|---|---|
| **Problem** | Clearly state the market problem or opportunity. |
| **Product / Service** | Explain your solution and product/service specifics. |
| **Target Customers** | Identify top 3 customers and why they'd buy. |
| **Market / Competition** | Describe market potential (with numbers) + ≥3 competitors. |
| **Research / Technology / Innovation** | Explain the technology; emphasize novelty. |
| **Team** | All founders + key employees: roles, strengths, experience. Key advisors/board. |
| **Financing** | Grants, awards, loans, investor funding, or none — with order of magnitude. |
| **Achievements** | Current R&D status + realized business development activities. |

## Step 4 — VK Application

### Academic Link *(eligibility requirement)*

Must satisfy **at least one**:

1. You or a co-founder are/were affiliated with a Swiss
   academic institution within the last **6 months**
   (enrolled or employed).
2. You have or plan a **research collaboration** with a
   Swiss academic partner → upload support letter
   (Step 5 or email info@venturekick.ch).

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Academic link 👁 | Select institution (ETH, EPFL, UNIGE, ZHAW, etc.) |
| Academic link description | Max **200 characters** |
| Development plan, use of funds, deliverables & milestones | Max **2 000 characters** |
| Sustainability | Product/service + internal operations. Max **2 000 characters** |

## Step 5 — Documents *(optional)*

Upload a **single PDF** (max 15 MB) combining:

- Up to **3 slides** summarizing the project
- Evidence of Swiss academic research collaboration
  (contract or support letter)
