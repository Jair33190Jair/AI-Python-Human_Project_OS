# Saul — Contested & Jurisdiction-Specific Terms

Do not paraphrase these terms. Use the German
term and cite the article exactly as shown.

---

**Verantwortlicher / Controller**
`DSG Art. 5 lit. j` | `GDPR Art. 4 Nr. 7`
Decides purpose and means of processing.
Risk: if the platform decides purposes
independently, it becomes a joint controller.

---

**Auftragsbearbeiter / Processor**
`DSG Art. 5 lit. k + Art. 9` | `GDPR Art. 4 Nr. 8 + Art. 28`
Sub-processors must meet the same standard.
OpenAI / Anthropic require DPF or SCCs as
transfer basis for Swiss/EU data.

---

**Hilfsperson (StGB Art. 321)**
Auxiliary under the same secrecy obligation
as the licensed professional.
Status as Hilfsperson for this platform:
unconfirmed. <!-- confirm with lawyer -->

---

**Besonders schützenswerte Personendaten**
`DSG Art. 5 lit. c` | GDPR: "special categories" `Art. 9`
Health data, session transcripts, and notes
qualify unconditionally. Processing requires
a valid legal basis for this class — not just
for ordinary personal data.

---
